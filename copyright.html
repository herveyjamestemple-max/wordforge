Copyright & takedowns

Report a rights concern.

This page gives a general reporting route for a future operating site. It is not a representation of a legally complete DMCA process or of any designated agent.

How to report allegedly infringing material

Send a clear report to the site owner using the real, monitored contact method that the owner must publish. That method has not yet been configured. Reports should identify the material precisely and provide enough information for a human reviewer to locate and assess it.

What to include

  • Your name and a reliable contact method.
  • A description of the copyrighted work or other right you believe is affected.
  • The exact WordForge page, project, or material at issue, and enough detail to locate it.
  • An explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized and any relevant supporting information.
  • A statement that your report is accurate to the best of your knowledge and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them.

Review and action

Once an actual reporting channel exists, the site operator may review the report, seek additional information, restrict or remove material where appropriate, and notify relevant parties where suitable. No reporting inbox or response timeline is active in this static version.

Responding to a mistaken removal

If material is restricted or removed in error, the affected person may contact the operator through its published method, identify the material and decision, explain the mistake, and provide supporting information. Further review depends on the operator configuring a process and contact channel.

Applicable law

Rights, notices, and responses are governed by applicable law. This general information is not legal advice and does not claim to satisfy every jurisdiction's notice, counter-notice, agent, or other requirements. Consult qualified counsel if needed.
