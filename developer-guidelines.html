Developer Rules

Good listings start with good information.

These requirements apply if WordForge enables project submissions in the future. The current site has no live submission channel.

Developer requirements

  • Submit only projects you own or are authorized to represent and distribute.
  • Respect copyright, trademarks, licenses, and attribution requirements, and provide ownership or authorization details when requested.
  • Provide an accurate project name, description, publisher identity, version, supported platforms, and update information. Correct details when they change.
  • Explain relevant permissions, data collection, sharing, storage, and privacy practices in clear language.
  • Link to legitimate official project and download sources. Clearly distinguish official destinations from any third-party sources.
  • Disclose known security risks, important technical requirements, and material limitations.
  • Do not bundle unrelated software without clear advance disclosure and consent where required.
  • Avoid deceptive advertising, fake reviews, exaggerated claims, and misleading download controls.
  • Respond to legitimate copyright, privacy, accuracy, or safety reports through the configured owner contact channel.
  • Keep listing information up to date or request removal when the project is no longer maintained or authorized.

Review and removal

Submissions may be declined or removed if they are incomplete, misleading, unauthorized, unsafe, unlawful, or inconsistent with the Community Guidelines. Future reviews would be handled according to a human process; WordForge does not claim automated moderation or security scanning.

No endorsement or guarantee

A listing does not constitute endorsement, certification, security verification, or a guarantee of safety, availability, or quality. Visitors should independently review a project's source, permissions, license, and download destination.

Submission status

There is no live way to submit a project through this website. See How submissions work and do not treat this static demonstration as a functioning intake service.
