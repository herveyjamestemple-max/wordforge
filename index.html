Future possibility
The vocabulary behind the app

Useful words matter more than a big number.

We want WordForge to grow into a large, useful English vocabulary resource. An exceptionally large collection—potentially approaching one billion word entries or forms—may be an ambition to explore, not a claim about a database that exists today or a confirmed delivery target.

As development progresses, the size, accuracy, licensing, and usefulness of any word collection will need to be verified. Reliable sources, clear definitions, helpful examples, and high-quality search matter more than an inflated word count.

Release roadmap

Every stage below is a plan. No release date is set, and no milestone is being presented as complete.
