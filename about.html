About WordForge

Words first. Built with care.

WordForge is planned as an English vocabulary and dictionary application that can grow into a broader language-learning and writing platform.

The product direction

The initial product direction is a useful English dictionary and vocabulary app. An Android release through Google Play is planned first; an iOS version for the Apple App Store is planned for a later stage. These are plans, not published releases, and no release date has been announced.

What exists today

The Android source project is an early introductory prototype. Its dictionary, vocabulary-learning, and writing features are not implemented. This website includes a small, separate browser vocabulary demo; its sample words and temporary practice controls are not part of the Android app.

Building a useful vocabulary resource

WordForge aims to develop a broad vocabulary resource over time. Accuracy, helpful definitions and examples, search quality, reliable sources, and licensing will be assessed as the project develops. Ambitions about a very large collection are not claims about an existing database.

A separate website directory

The Explore section is a front-end demonstration with fictional listings. It is separate from the WordForge learning app and does not currently accept submissions or verify projects.

Future product information

Release announcements, notes, feature documentation, frequently asked questions, and app support information can be added here as the product progresses. They are not currently published services, and this site has no contact or submission backend.
