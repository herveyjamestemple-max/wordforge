Community & content

Keep discovery useful and respectful.

These rules describe content that must not be submitted or promoted through WordForge. This version has no user-generated content system or automated moderation.

Prohibited content and conduct

  • Malware, ransomware, spyware, credential theft, phishing, or malicious files disguised as legitimate applications.
  • Stolen accounts or data, or tools intended to enable unauthorized access or abuse.
  • Fraud, impersonation, scams, deceptive downloads, misleading claims, fake reviews, or deceptive download buttons.
  • Copyright infringement, unauthorized redistribution, or other violations of intellectual-property rights.
  • Doxxing, harassment, threats, targeted abuse, or content intended to intimidate or endanger people.
  • Content or activity that violates applicable law, or attempts to exploit or compromise the WordForge platform.

Reporting

Identify the relevant page or project and explain the concern. For copyright reports, include the information described in our Copyright and Takedown Policy. For security concerns, see Security and Download Safety. Reports require a real owner contact method, which is not yet configured; see the Contact page.

Review, moderation, and removal

If a live submission feature is added, reports would be reviewed manually by the site operator. The operator may request details, restrict or remove content, or take other appropriate action. No automatic detection, response time, or moderation team is represented in this demonstration.

Appeals

A person who believes content was incorrectly restricted or removed should use the owner's published contact method and explain the specific decision and relevant evidence. An appeal is not available until a working contact channel and review process are configured.
