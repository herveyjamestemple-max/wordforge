Privacy Policy

A clear view of this static site.

This policy describes the current demonstration website. It must be updated before adding analytics, forms, cookies, advertising, or other data-collecting services.

Effective: October 24, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026

Information WordForge collects

The static site does not provide accounts, contact forms, submissions, analytics, advertising trackers, or a mechanism that sends your search query to a WordForge application server. Directory search and filtering operate in your browser. If you submit a search, its query may appear in the page URL and browser history; the hosting provider may process requested URLs in its logs.

The separate vocabulary browser preview uses a small sample word list and keeps its temporary “marked known” count only in the page's memory. That session progress is not saved or sent anywhere and disappears when you leave or reload the preview. The sample project and vocabulary data are included with the site files.

Do not submit personal or sensitive information through this demonstration. There is no message submission feature.

Hosting provider logs

When you visit a hosted website, the hosting provider may receive technical information such as an IP address, requested page, timestamp, browser information, and error details in server or network logs. The hosting provider determines its own processing, retention, and privacy practices. This policy does not claim that no information is processed anywhere.

External websites

If links to third-party sites are added or followed, those sites may collect information under their own privacy notices and practices. WordForge does not control them. Review their policies before sharing information.

Contact and submissions in the future

If the owner later adds an email contact, form, submission provider, analytics, cookies, or another service, this policy must be updated before that feature is enabled. The revised policy should identify what is collected, why, who processes it, retention, and available choices. No such feature is configured in this version.

Retention and deletion

This static site does not maintain a WordForge account or submission database, so it has no application-level user records to retain or delete. Hosting-provider logs may be retained under that provider's policies. If a contact or submission process is introduced, its retention and deletion arrangements must be described here before use.

Children's privacy

This site is not designed to collect information from children, and no account or submission facility is offered. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of third-party software and external sites.

Security limitations

Reasonable care should be taken to publish and maintain the site, but no website or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending confidential information to an unconfigured or untrusted channel.

Policy updates

Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Material changes should be made before newly introduced collection or processing begins.
