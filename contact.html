What to report

  • Incorrect listing: identify the page and explain which detail appears inaccurate.
  • Copyright concern: include the work, location, basis for the claim, and a response contact; see the copyright policy.
  • Security issue: identify the affected listing and describe the concern without sharing exploit instructions publicly.
  • Bug or accessibility issue: include the page, browser/device if relevant, and steps to reproduce.

Personal social profiles are not guaranteed reporting channels. Reports cannot be submitted or tracked through this website.
